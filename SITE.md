# English Learning Landing Page — Site Vision

## 1. Vision
An interactive English learning platform centered around flashcard-based study. The site empowers users to create, organize, and study vocabulary through an engaging, modern UI. The experience is designed to feel premium, educational, and encouraging.

## 2. Stitch Project
- **Project ID:** 17795544217897107469
- **Device Type:** Desktop
- **Design Theme:** Light mode, Lexend font, Round-8 corners, Custom color #1e1eae

## 3. Target Audience
English language learners of all levels who prefer self-paced, flashcard-based study methods.
