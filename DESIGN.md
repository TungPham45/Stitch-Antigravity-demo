# Design System: English Learning Landing Page
**Project ID:** 17795544217897107469

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The "English Learning Landing Page" features a **Clean, Trustworthy, and Educational** atmosphere. The aesthetic is modern and professional, utilizing a systematic blue-based visual language that evokes stability and focus. The interface balances high-density information (like statistics) with airy hero sections, creating a "Modern Academic" vibe that feels premium and accessible.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
*   **Deep Academic Blue (#1e1eae):** The primary brand color, used for critical actions (CTAs), primary icons, and interactive highlights.
*   **Deep Night Indigo (#16168a):** The hover state for primary elements, adding depth and feedback during interaction.
*   **Soft Cloud Gray (#f6f6f8):** The primary light mode background, providing a neutral and easy-to-read surface.
*   **Midnight Navy (#121220):** the primary dark mode background, maintaining high contrast and brand consistency.
*   **Pure White (#ffffff):** Used for elevated surfaces like cards, sidebars, and containers in light mode.
*   **Onyx Text (#121217):** The main color for headlines and primary body copy, ensuring maximum legibility.
*   **Slate Muted Purple (#656586):** Used for secondary text, descriptions, and labels to create visual hierarchy.

## 3. Typography Rules
*   **Font Family:** **Lexend**, a sans-serif typeface designed for clarity and reading speed, perfectly suited for an educational platform.
*   **Headers:** Bold weights (700-900) are used for hero headlines and section titles to command attention.
*   **Body:** Regular to medium weights (400-500) with generous line-height for comfortable long-form reading.
*   **Letter Spacing:** Subtle tight tracking for headers and standard tracking for body text to maintain a crisp look.

## 4. Component Stylings
*   **Buttons:**
    *   **Primary:** Solid **Deep Academic Blue (#1e1eae)** background with white text. They feature **rounded-full** or **generously rounded corners (0.75rem)**. Behavior includes a subtle scale up (`hover:scale-105`) and transition to **Deep Night Indigo (#16168a)** on hover.
    *   **Secondary:** Ghost or outlined styles with blue strokes, used for less urgent interactions.
*   **Cards/Containers:**
    *   Designed with **Generously rounded corners (0.75rem / rounded-2xl)**.
    *   Feature **Whisper-soft diffused shadows (shadow-sm)** to indicate elevation.
    *   Often include a **Subtle ring (ring-1 ring-slate-900/5)** for crisp definition against the background.
*   **Inputs/Forms:**
    *   Clean, minimalist stroke-based backgrounds that transition to a tinted ring on focus.

## 5. Layout Principles
*   **Whitespace:** The layout prioritizes **Generous breathing room** between sections to prevent cognitive overload.
*   **Alignment:** Utilizes a **Clean grid system** (max-width: 6xl) for content alignment, with a **Sticky Sidebar** on the left for persistent navigation.
*   **Composition:** A classic two-column hero layout transitions into three-card grids for features, ensuring a logical flow of information from high-level benefits to specific features.
